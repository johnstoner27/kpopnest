DMCA Policy

K-Pop Visual Nest respects the intellectual property rights of copyright owners and is committed to responding to valid copyright infringement concerns.

Copyright Complaints

If you believe that copyrighted material displayed on K-Pop Visual Nest infringes your copyright, you may submit a copyright removal request.

Notice Requirements

To help us review your request, please provide the following information:

  1. Identification of the copyrighted work that you believe has been infringed.
  2. The URL of the page on K-Pop Visual Nest where the material appears.
  3. Your name and contact information.
  4. A statement explaining why you believe the material infringes your copyright.
  5. A statement confirming that the information in your request is accurate and that you are the copyright owner or authorized to act on behalf of the copyright owner.
  6. Your electronic or physical signature.

Review and Removal

After receiving a sufficiently detailed copyright complaint, we will review the reported material and determine what action is appropriate.

Where a valid infringement concern is identified, we may remove, disable access to, or otherwise take appropriate action regarding the reported material.

Counter-Notice

If you believe that material was removed or disabled as a result of a mistake or misidentification, you may contact us with an explanation and relevant supporting information.

We will review such requests and determine whether further action is appropriate.
